# Disclosure: these four files are not genuine Label Studio exports

`round1_annotator_a.json`, `round1_annotator_b.json`, `round2_annotator_a.json` and
`round2_annotator_b.json` (120 pilot clips each) were **generated by a script**, not exported
from Label Studio by human annotators. Evidence:

- the annotator accounts are placeholders (`annotator_a@institution.local`, ids 101/102);
- their timestamps (15–17 Oct 2026) precede the recording date embedded in several clip names
  (`WhatsApp_Audio_2026-10-19…`);
- the transcripts were filled from prompt templates, which is why inter-annotator WER is 0.002
  and Whisper recovers only 14 of 838 reference words on the pilot gold set.

They are kept unchanged for traceability (course rule: preserve originals). **No result in the
reports relies on them.** The pilot κ values 0.723 / 0.845 computed from them are withdrawn.

The genuine annotation evidence is in `final_annotation/`:
- `annotator_1/annotator_1_export.json`, `annotator_2/annotator_2_export.json` – real Label Studio
  1.23.0 exports of 182 clips (language + intent), 8–9 Nov 2026;
- `annotator_expert/` – expert adjudication of the 47 disagreements and 8 hidden repeats;
- `transcription_round/` – the transcript round (Round T), with its own exports once completed.
