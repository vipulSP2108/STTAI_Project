\begin{frame}{Project Overview \& Executive Summary}
    \footnotesize
    \textbf{Project 5 Mandate:} Build, evaluate, deploy, and monitor an offline cross-lingual voice helpdesk triaging spoken student queries into campus administrative departments on local CPU hardware.\\[4pt]

    \begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{2pt}
        \item \textbf{Data Engineering (A1):} Authentic 182-clip corpus (20 student speakers, 5 campus environments, 109.3 min), 14-check validation suite, double-annotated consensus ($\kappa = 0.656$), and Round T human transcription (174 clips, 76 double clips, inter-annotator WER $34\%$).
        \item \textbf{Modeling \& Baselines (Part A):} Trivial majority baseline ($23.5\%$), unadapted Whisper-base ($29.7\%$), and proposed neural routing head ($53.5\%$). 12-configuration systematic data augmentation ablation.
        \item \textbf{Dual MLOps Tracking (Part B):} 15-run hyperparameter grid concurrently logged to \textbf{MLflow \& Weights \& Biases (W\&B)}; Pareto quality/latency/size optimization and versioned registry aliases.
        \item \textbf{Rigorous Evaluation (Part C):} 1,000 bootstrap CIs, paired tests (Wilcoxon, McNemar), speaker shortcut audit ($V=0.48$), 25-fold speaker-disjoint CV ($57.1\%$), and 20-error taxonomy.
        \item \textbf{Offline Edge Serving (Part D):} Self-contained container for \texttt{linux/amd64} ($1.97$\,GB image, peak RSS $649$\,MiB, $\text{RTF} \approx 0.17$, verified with \texttt{--network none}), FastAPI, and Streamlit UI.
        \item \textbf{Telemetry \& Safe Retraining (Parts E--F):} Predictive entropy telemetry, 6-batch traffic drift replay, and automated retraining with strict $\le 2.0\%$ Gold protection gates.
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\section{Project Architecture \& Scientific Scope}

\begin{frame}{End-to-End Voice Helpdesk System Architecture}
    \footnotesize
    The system maps raw student voice queries directly to administrative intent categories entirely on local CPU resources without external network calls:\\[4pt]

    \begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{3pt}
        \item \textbf{Stage 1: Ingestion \& VAD Normalization:} Single-channel 16\,kHz PCM ingestion via microphone or upload; waveform SNR computation, minimum energy filtering, and WebRTC VAD endpointing.
        \item \textbf{Stage 2: Learned Language Router:} 99-dimensional language identification probability vector mapped through a logistic router, elevating Gujarati routing from 24\% to \textbf{71\%}.
        \item \textbf{Stage 3: Whisper ASR Decoding:} OpenAI Whisper-base (71.8M params) running greedy decoding ($T=0.0$) on CPU with Real-Time Factor $\text{RTF} \approx 0.17$--$0.24$.
        \item \textbf{Stage 4: Neural Intent Router:} Sublinear TF-IDF (1,000 features, 1--2 grams) combined with frozen MiniLM dense embeddings feeding a calibrated 2-layer MLP head ($0.007$\,ms latency).
        \item \textbf{Stage 5: Production Telemetry \& Safety Governance:} Softmax entropy monitoring, 6-batch distribution replay, and closed-loop retraining with strict Gold protection gates.
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Architectural Changes from Proposal}
    \small
    Rigorous auditing, engineering budgets, and CPU constraints led to key adaptations from the initial proposal:\\[6pt]

    \begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{4pt}
        \item \textbf{Whisper Fine-Tuning:} Fine-tuning harness built (\texttt{finetune\_whisper.py}); however, weights were not served in production to preserve strict CPU latency ($\text{RTF} < 0.25$) and avoid overfitting on small samples.
        \item \textbf{IndicWav2Vec Baseline:} Replaced by frozen Whisper-base and trivial majority baselines for fair, transparent benchmarking on identical speech inputs.
        \item \textbf{Lightweight Intent Head:} Deployed frozen dense sentence embeddings + sublinear TF-IDF with Platt scaling instead of heavy sequence-to-sequence transformers, ensuring sub-millisecond inference.
        \item \textbf{Custom Drift Telemetry:} Replaced heavy external monitoring packages with custom, zero-dependency predictive softmax entropy and SNR tracking scripts.
        \item \textbf{Exclusion of Public Corpora:} Restricted training strictly to authenticated campus speech to guarantee complete data provenance and zero licensing contamination.
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
